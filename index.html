What is the difference between AEO, GEO and SEO?+

SEO helps your pages rank in Google results. AEO makes your content the direct answer to a question. GEO gets your business cited inside answers written by AI tools like ChatGPT. They work best together, since AI tools often pull from pages that already rank well.
